About Us

We're Forged Interactive! We take pride in every game we forge, and we're always hunting for new ideas in game design and development. It's taken a lot of trial and error to get here, and we're still learning with every project.

Our founder is also a metalsmith, so the name isn't just for show. We make games the same way we forge. We pride ourselves on our INNOVATION, our DRIVE, and our DEVOTION to creating quality games.

History

  • jan 1, 2021 Opened as Xepherfae Studios with our first official release, Spardyr Reimagined: Detention Imminent.
  • 2022 Rebranded as Forged Interactive! Same team, new name.
  • 2024 Won the SkillsUSA Kentucky Gold Award for game dev with the jam edition of To Hell and Back
  • now Forging our first commercial game, To Hell and Back!
